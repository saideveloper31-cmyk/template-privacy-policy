Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-04-28 (JST)

This Privacy Policy explains how Template ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the App. The App is a reflex-tap game that includes ad display and optional sound/settings customization.

1. Information We May Collect

The App may collect or process the following categories of information, depending on your device settings and usage:

  • Advertising identifiers (for example, IDFA on iOS or AAID on Android)
  • Device and technical information (OS version, app version, language, screen size)
  • Ad interaction data (ad request, impression, click, reward callback events)
  • Locally stored gameplay preferences (difficulty, game duration, sound on/off, sound volume)
  • Locally stored game progress (for example, best score)

We do not require account registration for this App.

2. How We Use Information

  • Serving and measuring ads
  • Preventing fraud and abuse in ad delivery
  • Providing game features and restoring your saved preferences on your device
  • Responding to inquiries and troubleshooting
  • Fulfilling legal obligations

3. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC) for advertising delivery and measurement
  • Apple App Tracking Transparency framework (iOS) for tracking permission handling

Gameplay preferences and best score are stored locally on your device using on-device storage APIs and are not uploaded by us to our own servers.

4. Legal Bases and Regional Rights

Depending on your region, we process data in accordance with applicable laws, including APPI (Japan), GDPR (EEA/UK, where applicable), and CCPA/CPRA (California, where applicable).

5. Data Retention

We keep information only for as long as necessary to provide the App, maintain security, and satisfy legal requirements.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age required by applicable law in your jurisdiction.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date on this page.
